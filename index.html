ABOUT ELFAMCO ELVITA

ELFAMCO ELVITA Multi Purpose Cooperative aims to support local farmers and strengthen the agricultural community in Narra, Palawan. Through cooperation and shared goals, we work toward better opportunities for our members and affordable products for the community.
